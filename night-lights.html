Night Lights Flash Cards

Practise identifying vessel lights at night for the RYA Day Skipper (COLREGs Part C, Rules 20–31). You see the lights, work out what the vessel is and which way she's heading, then turn the card over.

Quick reference — arcs of visibility (Rule 21)

MastheadWhite, 225° ahead. Vessels 50 m and over carry a second one, aft and higher.
SidelightsRed to port, green to starboard, each 112.5° from ahead to 22.5° abaft the beam.
SternlightWhite, 135° over the stern.
Towing lightYellow, same arc as the sternlight, fitted above it.
All-roundShows 360°. Used for anchor lights and for special-status signals such as NUC, RAM, fishing and pilot.
Based on the International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea (COLREGs), the rules taught in the RYA Day Skipper syllabus. This is a study aid only: check the RYA Day Skipper handbook or the full COLREGs text before relying on anything here.
